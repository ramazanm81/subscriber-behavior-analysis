Telecom Performance and Revenue Analytics
Project Overview

This project focuses on telecom KPI performance monitoring and subscriber revenue behavior analysis using interactive Tableau dashboards.

The objective is to design user-friendly, insight-driven dashboards that support marketing, CRM, and executive-level decision making.

The project consists of two major analytical components:

KPI Performance Analysis

Voice Revenue and Subscriber Behavior Analysis

All dashboards are developed using Tableau Desktop.

Part 1 – KPI Performance Analysis
Business Context

As a Data Analyst in a telecom company, frequent ad-hoc requests are received from marketing and CRM teams regarding tariff performance across multiple KPIs and time periods.

To streamline decision-making, dynamic dashboards were developed to provide compact, interactive, and insight-focused reporting.

Data Description

Part 2 – Voice Revenue and Subscriber Behavior Analysis
Business Context

Executive board identified a consistent decline in total voice duration.

Objective:
Identify behavioral and revenue-related patterns associated with voice decline.

The dataset contains 4 months of anonymized subscriber-level data.

Analytical Focus

Identify characteristics of subscribers whose voice duration declined and explore potential explanatory patterns.

Dashboard – Subscriber Profile Analysis
Key Investigations

Does voice duration decline more for subscribers with:

Higher MB usage?

Higher revenue?

Higher total minutes of usage?

Did voice duration decline across top 10 tariffs by voice revenue?

Which tariff experienced the highest decline in voice duration?

For Tariff 5:

Was decline stronger among subscribers with higher MB usage?

Are results consistent for subscribers whose voice duration decreased more than 50%?
